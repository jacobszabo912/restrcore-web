Who We Are

Built by Operators,
for Operators.

RestrCore was born out of frustration. Our small team brings over 20 years of combined experience across virtually every role in the restaurant industry — from bussing tables and working the line, to managing operations, scheduling staff, running the books, and being stakeholders in restaurants ourselves.

We have used every major POS system on the market. We have fought with systems that go down during a dinner rush, paid transaction fees that did not make sense, and spent Sunday mornings manually reconciling reports that should have taken minutes.

So we built what we always wanted — a system that actually thinks like a restaurant operator. One platform that connects every part of your business, runs locally so it is always fast, and gives you the financial visibility to make real decisions.

Our mission is simple: support small to medium independent restaurant operators by giving them powerful tools, the knowledge to use them, and a partner who actually understands their business.
